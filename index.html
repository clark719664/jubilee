Jubilee

Count the sleeps to every holiday.

Jubilee is a free holiday app for kids. It counts down to 76 celebrations from around the world, with games, stories, recipes and crafts to enjoy along the way.

    • No adsNothing is advertised to your child.

    • No accountsThere is no sign-up and no password.
    • No trackingNothing your child does leaves the device.
    • No links outKids can't tap their way out of the app.

    Put Jubilee on your home screen

    It opens full screen with its own icon, like any other app. You don't need an app store.

    You're in Facebook's browser, which can't install apps. Tap the menu at the top of the screen and choose Open in browser, then come back to this section.

    Jubilee is installed. Look for its icon on your home screen.

    iPhone or iPad

    1. Open this page in Safari.
    2. Tap the Share button.
    3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

    Android

    1. Open this page in Chrome.
    2. Tap the menu in the top corner.
    3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.

    Computer

    1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge.
    2. Click the install icon in the address bar.
    3. Click Install.
